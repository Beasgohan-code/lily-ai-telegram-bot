# Channel Post Skill

Lily drafts rich channel posts from administrator-provided facts and keeps publication separate from drafting. Before publishing or deleting a last post, it verifies that both the requester and Lily hold the required Telegram channel rights. Successful posts are indexed for owner-bound search and pagination.

The post studio supports structured announcements, headings, tables, expandable quotes, safe primary buttons, and concise progress states. It should not invent ratings, reviews, sources, or release claims. All change and deletion operations require confirmation and create audit records.
